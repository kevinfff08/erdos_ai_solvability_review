## 原问题

Let $h(x)$ count the number of integers $1\leq a<b<x$ such that $(a,b)=1$ and $\sigma(a)=\sigma(b)$, where $\sigma$ is the sum of divisors function.

Is it true that $h(x)>x^{2-o(1)}$?
